KATARASU AI

Desktop content production and publishing software for independent music creators.

About

KATARASU AI helps music creators prepare visual content, short-form videos, release metadata and publishing assets from their original music.

Creators remain in control of their content and explicitly review and approve publishing actions before content is sent to a connected platform.

How it works

1. The creator provides their own music and visual assets.

2. KATARASU AI prepares release visuals and short-form video.

3. The creator connects their own supported platform account.

4. Publishing information is presented for review.

5. Content is sent only after explicit creator confirmation.

TikTok Integration

KATARASU AI uses TikTok Login Kit to allow creators to authorize their own TikTok account.

The TikTok Content Posting API is used to send creator-selected video content after the creator reviews the publishing settings and explicitly confirms the action.
