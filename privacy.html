KATARASU BOT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Overview

KATARASU BOT is a personal content management and publishing tool used to prepare, manage, and publish original Katarasu music and video content to supported platforms.

2. Information Accessed

When KATARASU BOT is connected to a third-party platform through OAuth or another official authorization mechanism, the application may receive authorization tokens and basic account information necessary to identify the connected account and perform authorized publishing functions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through connected platforms is used only to provide the functionality requested by the authorized user, including account authentication, content upload, publishing, and management of content.

4. Authorization Tokens

Authorization tokens may be stored locally by the application so that the authorized user does not need to sign in again for every operation. These credentials are used only to communicate with the relevant platform's official API.

5. Data Sharing

KATARASU BOT does not sell personal information. Information obtained through connected platform APIs is not sold or provided to third parties for advertising purposes.

6. Third-Party Services

KATARASU BOT may communicate with third-party platforms using their official APIs. Information processed by those platforms is also subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored authorization credentials may be removed when access to a connected platform is no longer required. Users may also revoke application access through the account or security settings provided by the relevant third-party platform.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and application data. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KATARASU BOT functionality or its use of connected platform APIs changes.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to the operator of KATARASU BOT through the official Katarasu channels.
